Biography

Dr. Jennifer Chen is Professor of Early Childhood Education at Kean University and a Senior Research Fellow at NIEER, Rutgers University. She is a well-respected scholar who has authored or co-authored more than 100 scholarly publications, including four books, co-edited volumes, and numerous refereed research articles. Her research threads multiple strands of early childhood education (ECE), including child development, language and literacy, teacher stress and thriving, and the role of AI in young children's learning. Dr. Chen developed the pioneering POWER (Purposeful, Optimal, Wise, Ethical, Responsible) framework to guide ethical and developmentally appropriate AI practices in ECE.

Scales & Permission to Use

If you are interested in using any of the following scales for research, clinical work, and/or teaching purposes, please contact Dr. Jennifer Chen at jchen@kean.edu.

1. Teacher Stress Scale (TSS)

Chen, J. J., Li, Z., Rodrigues, W., & Kaufman, S. (2022)

Measures teaching-related stress and inadequate school-based support among educators.

APA PsycTests (ID: 999985552) | Validation Study (Frontiers)

2. Teacher Thriving Scale (TTS)

Chen, J. J., Li, Z., Rodrigues, W., & Kaufman, S. (2022)

Assesses teachers' capacity to thrive despite stress across adaptability, personal strengths, and positive mindset.

APA PsycTests (ID: 999985551) | Validation Study (Frontiers)

3. Perceived Parental Academic Support Scale (PPASS)

Chen, J. J.-L. (2005)

Measures student self-perceptions of academic encouragement, resources, and involvement provided by parents.

APA PsycTests (ID: 999902755) | Journal Monograph

4. Perceived Teacher Academic Support Scale (PTASS)

Chen, J. J.-L. (2005)

Evaluates students' perceived academic support, guidance, and high expectations from their teachers.

APA PsycTests (ID: 999902756) | Journal Monograph

5. Perceived Friend/Peer Academic Support Scale (PFASS)

Chen, J. J.-L. (2005)

Measures peer encouragement, study collaboration, and positive academic influences within friend networks.

APA PsycTests (ID: 999902757) | Journal Monograph

6. Perceived Academic Engagement Scale (PAES)

Chen, J. J.-L. (2005)

Quantifies student self-perceptions of academic motivation, classroom involvement, and study habits.

APA PsycTests (ID: 999902758) | Journal Monograph
